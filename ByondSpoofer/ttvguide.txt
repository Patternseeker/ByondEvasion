////////////////////////////////////////////////

This guide should yield 1x 50k points TTV and around 3-4 leftover bombs.

DISCLAIMER: Don't make leftover bombs as non-antag. You'll get banned for using them in station and antags know what a fully assembled
TTV bomb looks like. Miners won't make the best of it as well. Simply vent the remaining gasses inside the chamber and hide the pumps.

Leftover bombs have no research value but have comparable explosive strength. Useful for antagging.
Number of leftover bombs are limited to the amount of spare O2 you have. You can use every transfer valve if you have a fifth O2 canister.

ANTAG TIPS:
The TTV bomb itself is similar to the black-box in terms of bulky size and the suspicion it arises when seen. Keep this in mind.
Always carry bombs outside the toxins room wrapped in packaging. This makes them impossible to discern without opening.
Make sure the station does not get 50k points if you are an antag.
TTV bombs pair well with anything related to bluespace. Immovable rod launchers, bags of holding, bluespace compressors, bluespace bodybags,
bluespace launchpads etc. make delivery much more easier and effective.
You can get creative with the triggers just like how its done using grenades.
Usually, the only evidence that bombing leaves behind is eye witnesses.
Atmospherics + Incinerator is also very well suited for mass-producing 50K bombs but don't attempt this without some atmos tech experience.

////////////////////////////////////////////////

Things needed:
RPED, wrench, crowbar, screwdriver, analyzer - found in Toxins lab, tool storage, Sci techfab
2x T2 lasers (High-Powered-Micro-Laser) - found in Sci techfab, needs research first
2x freezer, 1x tank transfer valve - found in Toxins lab (some maps have the second freezer elsewhere within Science dept.)
2x air container (o2 and plasma) - found in Toxins storage
4x O2 canisters, 1x Plasma canister - found in Toxins storage
2x blue pumps, 1x scrubber - found in Toxins storage, Toxins lab

The setup (n for "any amount"):
INPUT = Connector -> Pipe (n) -> Gas pump -> Pipe (n) -> Injector
OUTPUT = Connector <- Volume pump <- Pipe(n) <- Manifold (attached to Freezer) <- Pipe (n) <- Scrubber

SPARE O2 (build elsewhere in lab) = Connector -> Volume pump -> Connector //// Freezer -> Connector

1) Acquire your tools and complete the setup. No roundstart setup fulfills the demands.
2) Make sure the interior door is bolted and the air alarm is set (to Scrubbing, Extended, Tritium).
3) Set the input gas pump to Max (4500 kPA).
4) Start putting in 3x O2 canisters in the chamber. Switch them out once they are empty.
4.5) While waiting, go print the lasers (research Industrial Engineering if its not done by this time).
5) Assembly your freezers. One should be streamlined to output and the other should be somewhere else in the room piped to a connector.
6) Set both freezers to 43k cooling. Turn them ON.
7) By this time the 3x O2 canisters should be emptied into the chamber.
8) Set the input gas pump to 40 kPA.
9) Connect in the Plasma canister.
10) Immediately hit the "igniter" button on the wall.

///// You are now starting a Tritium reaction and pumping the results into the output. This takes some time. /////

11) Connect the fourth oxygen canister to the second freezer and wait till it cools to 43.15 Kelvin
12) Pump the canister into the a blue pump using the other setup. Set the blue pumps settings to (In, Max kPA) but leave it off.

13) Empty one O2 container and one Plasma container into a scrubber. (Make sure to pick O2 in the scrubber menu)
14) Set the empty Plasma container aside.
15) Put the O2 container into the second empty blue pump. Set its settings to (In, Max kPA) but leave it off.
16) Attach the empty blue pump into the output.

///// Your tritium is done when the pipes arent going up in temperature anymore - analyze them a lot /////

17) Once the Tritium is done, un-check Tritium in the air alarm. -DON'T SET TO PLASMA YET-
18) Make absolutely sure the pipe contents are 43.15k. Turn on the volume pump when it is.
19) Wait until it finished pumping. You should have 0.0 mols in the pipes and a stagnant number of mols in the volume pump.
20) Unwrench the blue pump.
21) Turn off and unwrench the volume pump. Wrench it back in. Make sure the whole output pipenet has 0 mols of Tritium.
22) Turn on the blue pump. It should fill the O2 container with 800-806 kPA of Tritium. Turn it off and take the container out.
23) Wrench the blue pump back in. Make sure its contents are empty.

///// At this point you will harvest the plasma /////

24) Make sure the freezer is absolutely, positively turned off.
25) Set air alarm to (to Scrubbing, Extended, Plasma).
26) Wrench in the pump with the Plasma container.
27) Turn on the volume pump.
28) Turn on the blue pump, fill container with Plasma, turn off. Retreive the fully pressurized plasma container.

///// Time to add in the %66 O2 in the container /////

29) Take the O2 container and put it in the blue pump filled with O2.
30) Fill the rest of the container with O2 (remember, In - Max kPA). Close the pump and retreive container.
31) Insert both Plasma and O2 containers into a tank transfer valve.
32) Take two remote signallers. CHANGE THE FREQUENCY AND/OR CODE ON BOTH. Use screwdriver on one of them and attach to the TTV.

///// At this point you are holding on a mini-nuke. /////

33) Go to the launch site, turn on the microphone of the intercomm next to the Tachyon array.
34) Drop the TTV into the mass driver, hit the mass driver button.
35) Confirm with camera that the bomb is in the test site.
36) Send signal with the signaller on you, et voila.
